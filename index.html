System Effectiveness

How additional power allocation affects each system's performance. Power levels are not capped at 100% — systems can be overdriven well beyond it, at a coolant cost (see the Thermal & Power Guide).

SystemEffect of Power Increase
ReactorPower generation increases effectiveness, with diminishing returns.
BeamsRate of fire scales linearly.
MissilesReload speed scales linearly.
ManoeuvringRotation speed scales linearly.
ImpulseForward/backward speed scales linearly.
WarpLinear for power drain; non-linear in practical use.
Shield GeneratorRecharge speed scales linearly; defensive return is non-linear.
ScannerScanner range and alignment time scale linearly; increased power dramatically increases drone range.
Cargo DocksInter-dock transfer speed and tractor beam movement scale linearly.
